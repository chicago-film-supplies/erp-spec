Every shift has exactly one causal job. Other work performed during that shift is a **byproduct**
and absorbs zero incremental cost — it rode along on a crew-day already being paid for. The causal
job bears the whole crew-day.

Settled. The open part is whether long-haul should additionally absorb a byproduct-loss premium,
because a long-haul crew-day cannot pick up byproducts at all (HOT-004 / OQ-006).
